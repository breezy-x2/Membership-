Access thousands of books, journals, and digital resources with a single student membership. Apply online in minutes and get instant access.

What is the online Membership Card?

The Greenview Library Online Membership Card is your digital pass to everything our library offers. No more paper forms or long queues — your membership lives online and is linked to your student profile. Once registered, you can borrow books, reserve titles, access e-books and academic journals, and manage your account from anywhere at any time. Your digital card is valid for the full academic year and can be renewed online when it expires. Greenview Library
